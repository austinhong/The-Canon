What it does

  1. Sign in with campus SSO. Only enrolled students can see walks.
  2. Set a start, a destination, and a leave-by time.
  3. Match someone whose route overlaps yours in that window.
  4. If nobody is going your way, hand the same trip to the campus escort service.

The gap

Courses, housing, and dining already have software. The walk home after the library closes does not. Group chats are noisy. Escort services feel like a big ask. Consumer safety apps keep a history and are not limited to students.

Lantern is the short-lived, college-verified layer between those.
